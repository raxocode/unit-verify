## Ambiguous Units

Certain common unit terms in English refer to conflicting definitions across systems (such as US Customary vs Imperial, or metric tonne vs short ton). Rather than guessing silently, the engine rejects them with `AMBIGUOUS_UNIT` and provides deterministic choices:

| Input Term | Reason | Deterministic Choices |
|---|---|---|
| `ton`, `tons` | Metric tonne vs US short ton vs Imperial long ton | `['tonne', 'ton_us', 'ton_uk']` |
| `gallon`, `gallons` | US liquid gallon (3.785 L) vs Imperial gallon (4.546 L) | `['gal_us', 'gal_uk']` |
| `fl oz`, `fluid ounce`, `fluid ounces` | US fluid ounce vs UK fluid ounce | `['gal_us', 'gal_uk']` |
